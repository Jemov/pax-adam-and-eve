# Карта / Map

Природная среда примерно 6000 лет назад (около 4000 до н. э.) — основа художественного сценария. Береговые линии, озёра, биомы и морской лёд обобщены для глобальной игровой карты. Это не точная реконструкция конкретного года.

The fictional scenario uses an approximate environment around 6000 years ago (4000 BCE). Coastlines, lakes, biomes and sea ice are generalized for a global game map, not a precise reconstruction of one year.

- **Основа / Base:** [Natural Earth II](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/50m-raster-data/50m-natural-earth-2/) and Natural Earth 1:10m land/lakes. [Public domain](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). Derived texture 4096×2048; vegetation and palaeolakes modified for this scenario. No game texture is redistributed.
- **Климат / Climate:** [IPCC AR6 WG1, chapter 2](https://www.ipcc.ch/report/ar6/wg1/chapter/chapter-2/). The +0.2–1.0 °C range concerns the warmest mid-Holocene century relative to 1850–1900. It is background information, not measured local temperature or a uniform increase applied to provinces. Modern warming has exceeded that reference range.
- **Зелёная Сахара / Green Sahara:** [NOAA, African Humid Period](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/sites/default/files/2021-11/5%20End%20of%20the%20Africian%20Humid%20Period%20-Final_OCT%202021.pdf). Steppe/savanna is a broad schematic mosaic, not a dense rainforest across all North Africa.
- **Мега-Чад / Mega-Chad:** [NASA Earth Observatory](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/remnants-of-an-ancient-lake-146304/) and [Armitage et al., PNAS 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26124133/). Drawn near 350,000 km²; the polygon is hand generalized, not a digitized surveyed shoreline.
- **Туркана / Turkana:** [Nature 1982](https://www.nature.com/articles/298523a0). Larger lake shown schematically; its drawn area is a game design choice rather than a measured value for 4000 BCE.
- **Персидский залив / Persian Gulf:** [Lambeck 1996](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0012821X96000696). A modest northern embayment is schematic; local sea-level, sediment and coast histories are uncertain.
- **Балтика / Baltic:** [Miettinen et al. 2004](https://tuhat.helsinki.fi/ws/portalfiles/portal/23234786/Miettinen_et_al_2004_Quaternary_International.pdf). Only a schematic Bothnian coastal change is drawn. Regional uplift and a complete Littorina shoreline reconstruction are not simulated.
- **Аравия / Arabia:** wetter terrain and an illustrative southern palaeolake; no exact historical shoreline or lake area is claimed.
- **Лёд / Ice:** Greenland and Antarctic land ice remain. Sea ice is a schematic annual visual cycle, with northern maximum near March and southern maximum near September. The masks are not palaeoclimate observations. Water under sea ice remains water for gameplay. No continental ice sheet is added over Canada or Scandinavia.

Новые озёра меняют водную маску и площадь затронутых провинций в этой партии. Игровой граф соседства провинций сохраняется; детальная модель древних речных путей и локальной погоды не добавляется. Сезонный лёд не создаёт сухопутных мостов.

Added lakes update the campaign water mask and affected province areas. The game's province adjacency graph remains; detailed palaeoriver routing and local weather are not added. Seasonal ice does not create land bridges.
